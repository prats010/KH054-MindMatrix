# Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan (GKRA)

## 1. Overview
The Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan (GKRA) is a massive rural public works scheme introduced by the Government of India to empower and provide livelihood opportunities to the returnee migrant workers and rural citizens.

## 2. Benefits
- Focuses on 125 days of guaranteed employment in rural areas.
- Public works include the construction of Gram Panchayat Bhawans, Anganwadi Centres, National Highway works, Railway works, and Water Conservation projects.
- Provides immediate livelihood support to those who lost jobs due to economic distress.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Available to returnee migrant workers and rural citizens in need of employment.
- Minimum age of 18 years.
- Concentrated in 116 districts across 6 specific States: Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Jharkhand, and Odisha.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card.
- Bank Account details (for wage transfer).
- Job Card (MGNREGA job card).

## 5. Application Process
- Workers can register for a job card at their local Gram Panchayat.
- The scheme is implemented in a mission mode across various ministries coordinating with State Governments.
