# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

## 1. Overview
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) is a scheme launched by the Hon'ble Prime Minister for providing loans up to ₹10 Lakhs to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises.

## 2. Benefits
- MUDRA loans do not require collateral security.
- Categorized into three stages based on the stage of growth/funding needs:
  - **Shishu**: Loans up to ₹50,000.
  - **Kishore**: Loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5,000,000.
  - **Tarun**: Loans above ₹5,000,000 and up to ₹10,00,000.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Any Indian citizen who has a business plan for a non-farm sector income generating activity.
- The business must belong to manufacturing, processing, trading, or service sector.
- Age 18 years and above.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar / Voter ID / PAN Card (Identity Proof).
- Proof of Address.
- Business Address Proof and Licenses (if applicable).
- Bank Account details.

## 5. Application Process
- Applications can be submitted online at the Udyamimitra portal (udyamimitra.in).
- Alternatively, approach any authorized Commercial Bank, RRB, Small Finance Bank, or MFI with a business plan.
