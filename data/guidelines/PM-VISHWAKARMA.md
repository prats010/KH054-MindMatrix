# PM Vishwakarma Yojana

## 1. Overview
PM Vishwakarma is a Central Sector Scheme launched to provide end-to-end holistic support to traditional artisans and craftspeople scaling up their conventional products and services.

## 2. Benefits
- **Recognition:** PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card.
- **Skill Upgradation:** Basic training of 5-7 days and advanced training of 15+ days, with a training stipend of ₹500 per day.
- **Toolkit Incentive:** Up to ₹15,000 grant.
- **Credit Support:** Collateral-free credit up to ₹3 Lakhs (₹1 Lakh in the first tranche, ₹2 Lakhs in the second) at a concessional interest rate of 5%.
- **Marketing Support:** Quality certification, branding, e-commerce linkage, and trade fair participation.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools in one of the 18 family-based traditional trades (e.g., Carpenter, Boat Maker, Blacksmith, Goldsmith, Potter, Sculptor, Cobbler, Mason, Basket Maker, Barber, Tailor).
- Minimum age of 18 years on the date of registration.
- Should be engaged in the trade concerned on the date of registration.
- Should not have availed loans under similar schemes like PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, or MUDRA in the past 5 years.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card.
- Bank Account Details.
- Ration Card (for family identification).
- Proof of trade (self-declaration).

## 5. Application Process
- Free registration can be done online through Common Service Centres (CSCs) on the PM Vishwakarma portal.
- Three-step verification process involves Gram Panchayat, District Implementation Committee, and Screening Committee.
