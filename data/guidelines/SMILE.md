# Support for Marginalized Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise (SMILE)

## 1. Overview
SMILE is an umbrella scheme by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment designed to provide comprehensive rehabilitation for marginalized individuals, specifically focusing on Transgender persons and persons engaged in the act of begging.

## 2. Benefits
- **Scholarships:** For Transgender students from Class IX till post-graduation.
- **Skill Development:** Vocational training under PM-DAKSH to promote employability.
- **Medical Care:** Comprehensive Medical Health package in convergence with PM-JAY.
- **Housing:** Support for setting up 'Garima Greh' (Shelter Homes) providing food, clothing, and medical support.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a Transgender person.
- Holding a Transgender Certificate/Identity Card issued under the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019.
- No specific income limit is enforced for basic rehabilitation, though specific components (like scholarships) may have criteria.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card.
- Transgender Identity Certificate.
- Bank Account Details.

## 5. Application Process
- Apply online via the National Portal for Transgender Persons (transgender.dosje.gov.in).
- Beneficiaries can directly register for the PM-DAKSH skill training program via the same portal.
