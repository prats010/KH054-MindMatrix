# Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## 1. Scheme Objective
To provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all houseless households and those households living in kutcha and dilapidated house in rural areas by 2024.

## 2. Benefits
- Financial assistance of ₹1.20 lakh in plain areas and ₹1.30 lakh in hilly states, difficult areas, and IAP districts.
- Beneficiary is also entitled to 90/95 person-days of unskilled labor wage under MGNREGA.
- An additional assistance of ₹12,000 is provided for the construction of toilets under Swachh Bharat Mission-Gramin (SBM-G).

## 3. Identification of Beneficiaries
Identification of beneficiaries is based on the housing deprivation parameters of Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC), 2011, subject to 13-point exclusion criteria, followed by Gram Sabha verification.

Households that are completely houseless or living in zero, one, or two room houses with kutcha wall and kutcha roof are eligible.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria
Households are excluded if they have:
- Motorized two/three/four wheelers / agricultural equipment.
- Kisan Credit Card with a credit limit of ₹50,000 or above.
- Any member as a government employee.
- Households with non-agricultural enterprises registered with the government.
- Any member of the family earning more than ₹10,000 per month.
- Paying income tax or professional tax.
- Owning a refrigerator or landline phone.
- Owning 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land with at least one irrigation equipment.

## 5. Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank Account Details
- Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) number (if available)
- MGNREGA Job Card number (if available)
- Land Proof (document showing ownership of land where house is to be constructed)
- Income Certificate
