# Naya Savera Scheme

## 1. Overview
The "Naya Savera" (Free Coaching and Allied Scheme) is implemented by the Ministry of Minority Affairs. It aims to empower the students belonging to minority communities (Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis, and Jains) by preparing them for competitive examinations.

## 2. Benefits
- Provides free coaching for qualifying examinations for admission into technical/professional courses (Engineering, Medical, Law, Management, etc.).
- Coaching for competitive examinations for recruitment to Group 'A', 'B', and 'C' services and other equivalent posts under the Central and State Governments.
- Stipend of ₹2,500 per month for outstation students and ₹1,500 for local students for the duration of the coaching.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Candidate must belong to a notified Minority Community.
- Total family income from all sources should not exceed ₹6,00,000 per annum.
- Should have secured the requisite percentage of marks in the qualifying examination.
- Benefits can only be availed once by a student.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card.
- Income Certificate.
- Minority/Community Certificate (Self-declaration).
- Educational Certificates (Marksheets of qualifying exams).

## 5. Application Process
- The scheme is implemented through empaneled Coaching Institutions/Organizations.
- Students must apply directly to the empaneled coaching institutes based on advertisements published in local/national newspapers.
