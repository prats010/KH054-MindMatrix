# Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

## 1. Overview
Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a Maternity Benefit Programme that is implemented in all the districts of the country in accordance with the provision of the National Food Security Act, 2013.

## 2. Benefits
- Cash incentive of ₹5,000 in three instalments (₹1,000, ₹2,000, and ₹2,000).
- Eligible beneficiaries also receive cash incentive under Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) for institutional delivery.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- All Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM), excluding those in regular employment with the Central Government/State Governments/PSUs.
- Must be for the first living child of the family.
- Minimum age of 19 years.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card of the beneficiary.
- MCP (Mother and Child Protection) Card.
- Bank or Post Office Account details.

## 5. Application Process
1. Register at the Anganwadi Centre (AWC) / approved Health facility.
2. Fill up Form 1-A with necessary documents within 150 days of Last Menstrual Period (LMP).
3. Claims for the second and third instalments are submitted using Form 1-B and Form 1-C respectively.
