# Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

## 1. Overview
Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) is a safe motherhood intervention under the National Health Mission (NHM). It is being implemented with the objective of reducing maternal and neonatal mortality by promoting institutional delivery among poor pregnant women.

## 2. Benefits
- **LPS (Low Performing States):** Cash assistance up to ₹1,400 for mothers delivering in public health institutions. ASHA workers receive ₹600.
- **HPS (High Performing States):** Cash assistance up to ₹700 for mothers delivering in public health institutions. ASHA workers receive ₹600.
- Applicable in both rural and urban areas (urban incentives are slightly different).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- All pregnant women delivering in government health centres like Sub Centres, PHCs/CHCs/FRUs/general wards of District and state Hospitals.
- In HPS, the benefit is restricted to BPL/SC/ST women. In LPS, it is available to all women regardless of BPL/APL status.
- Minimum age of 19 years.

## 4. Required Documents
- Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card.
- Aadhaar Card.
- Bank Account Details.
- BPL/Caste Certificate (if applicable in HPS).

## 5. Application Process
- Register with the local ASHA worker or at the nearest Anganwadi Centre/Primary Health Centre as early as possible during the pregnancy.
- The cash incentive is disbursed to the beneficiary's bank account directly.
