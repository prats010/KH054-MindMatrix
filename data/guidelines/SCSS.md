# Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS)

## 1. Overview
The Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) is a government-backed retirement benefits program. It offers Indian residents over the age of 60 a regular income and is considered a safe investment option.

## 2. Benefits
- Provides high interest rates (currently around 8.2% p.a., subject to quarterly review).
- Investments qualify for tax deductions under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.
- Maximum deposit limit is ₹30 Lakhs.
- Tenure of 5 years, extendable by another 3 years.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- An individual aged 60 years or more.
- Retirees aged 55 years or more but less than 60 years (under VRS or Superannuation) can also open an account subject to specific conditions.
- Retired personnel of Defence Services (excluding Civilian Defence employees) can open an account upon attaining the age of 50 years.

## 4. Required Documents
- Age proof (Passport, PAN Card, Birth Certificate).
- Aadhaar Card.
- Two passport size photographs.

## 5. Application Process
- Fill the SCSS application form available at Post Offices or authorized banks.
- Submit the form with KYC documents and deposit amount.
