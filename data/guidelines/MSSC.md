# Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC)

## 1. Overview
The Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC) is a one-time small savings scheme designed specifically for women and girls, introduced in the 2023 Union Budget to promote financial independence.

## 2. Benefits
- Attractive fixed interest rate of 7.5% per annum, compounded quarterly.
- Partial withdrawal of up to 40% of the eligible balance allowed after one year.
- Maximum deposit limit up to ₹2 Lakh for a 2-year tenure.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Open to all women and girls of any age.
- Accounts for minors can be opened by the natural or legal guardian.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card.
- PAN Card.
- KYC documents as per banking norms.

## 5. Application Process
- Visit the nearest Post Office or participating bank.
- Submit the application form along with KYC documents and deposit amount.
