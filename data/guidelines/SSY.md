# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## 1. Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a small deposit scheme of the Government of India meant exclusively for a girl child and is launched as a part of the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign.

## 2. Benefits
- Provides one of the highest interest rates among small savings schemes (currently 8.2% p.a., subject to quarterly revision).
- Tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to ₹1.5 Lakh.
- Interest earned and maturity amount are completely tax-free.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- The account can be opened by the natural or legal guardian in the name of a girl child.
- The girl child must be 10 years of age or younger.
- Maximum of two accounts allowed per family (one for each girl child).

## 4. Required Documents
- Birth Certificate of the girl child.
- Aadhaar Card of the parent/guardian.
- Address Proof of the parent/guardian.

## 5. Application Process
- Visit any authorized post office or commercial bank branch.
- Fill the SSY account opening form (Form-1).
- Submit the required documents and initial deposit amount (minimum ₹250).
