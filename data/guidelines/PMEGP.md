# Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)

## 1. Overview
PMEGP is a major credit-linked subsidy programme aiming at generating self-employment opportunities through establishment of micro-enterprises in the non-farm sector by helping traditional artisans and unemployed youth.

## 2. Benefits
- Subsidy of 15% to 35% on the project cost depending upon the area (urban/rural) and beneficiary category.
- Maximum project cost of ₹50 Lakhs for the manufacturing sector and ₹20 Lakhs for the business/service sector.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Any individual above 18 years of age.
- For projects costing above ₹10 Lakhs (manufacturing) and ₹5 Lakhs (business/service), the beneficiary should possess at least VIII standard pass educational qualification.
- Only new projects are considered for sanction under PMEGP.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card.
- Highest Educational Qualification Certificate.
- Project Report (Detailed Business Plan).
- Rural area certificate (if applicable).

## 5. Application Process
- Apply online at the PMEGP e-Portal (kviconline.gov.in).
- The applications are evaluated by District Level Task Force Committees (DLTFC).
- Approved cases are forwarded to banks for credit decisions.
