# Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

## 1. Overview
Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) is a centrally sponsored scheme. It provides a health cover of ₹5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across public and private empaneled hospitals in India.

## 2. Target Beneficiaries
The scheme targets over 12 crore poor and vulnerable entitled families (approximately 50 crore beneficiaries) across the country.

## 3. Eligibility and Inclusion Criteria
The scheme does not have an enrollment process. It is an entitlement-based scheme. Families included are based on the deprivation and occupational criteria of the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011) for rural and urban areas respectively.

**General Income Criteria for Auto-Inclusion (Proxy):**
Households with an annual family income of less than ₹3,00,000 are generally considered within the deprivation criteria, though exact inclusion is strictly mapped against the SECC database.

There is NO cap on family size, age, or gender.

## 4. Key Benefits
- Provides ₹5 Lakhs per family, per year.
- Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses such as diagnostics and medicines.
- Cashless and paperless access to services at the point of care.
- Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one.

## 5. Required Documents at Hospital
Beneficiaries need to prove their identity at the empaneled hospital using:
- Aadhaar Card
- Ration Card (for family mapping)
- Income Certificate (if verification is required by state mandate)
