# Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)

## 1. Overview
Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) is a pension scheme exclusively for senior citizens aged 60 years and above, offering an assured return to provide financial security during old age.

## 2. Benefits
- Assured pension with a fixed return (around 7.4% per annum) for a policy term of 10 years.
- Pension can be availed on a monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly basis.
- Loan facility is available after completion of 3 policy years (up to 75% of the purchase price).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum Entry Age: 60 years (completed).
- Maximum Entry Age: No limit.
- Must be an Indian citizen.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card / Identity Proof.
- Age Proof (PAN Card, Passport, etc.).
- Bank Account details.

## 5. Application Process
- The scheme is operated by Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC).
- Eligible senior citizens can apply for the scheme online through the LIC website or offline by visiting an LIC branch.
